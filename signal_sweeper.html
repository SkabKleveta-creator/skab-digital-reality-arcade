SIGNAL SWEEPER

ARCADE GRID REPAIR

Clean. Restore. Survive. You are the repair agent inside a live AI chamber. Sweep corruption, collect signal, restore nodes, and break out through the exit before correction drones close the room.

Core Loop
Move → Collect → Sweep → Restore → Exit
Keyboard
Move: WASD/Arrows
A: Z/Space Sweep
B: X/Shift Dash
Touch
Left thumb pad moves. Right A sweeps/restores. B dashes.
Objective
Clear 5 escalating grids. Collect signal, restore every node, reach EXIT.
Press Enter / Space / A to begin

OBJECTIVE

GRID REPAIR REMINDER

A / Sweep Pulse clears corruption, stuns drones, and restores a node when you are close enough and have enough signal.

B / Dash is a short emergency burst. Use it to cross scanner cones, not to fly through the whole chamber.

The exit opens after all required nodes are restored. Follow the cyan objective arrow if you lose the path.
