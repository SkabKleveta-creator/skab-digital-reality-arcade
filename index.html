Capability View

What this work demonstrates

The Arcade is fun on the surface, but the deeper value is the repeatable way concepts move from rough idea to playable proof.

Back to top

Browser-first delivery

Single-page games and demos that run through simple links, making proof easier to share, test, and revise without a platform build.

Input discipline

Phone-first and keyboard-friendly controls that keep the player focused on the loop instead of learning a control scheme.

Validation as gameplay

Several builds treat evidence, repair, routing, handoffs, and AAR as the actual win condition instead of background paperwork.

Operational translation

Abstract problems become playable: supply flow, audit controls, broken handoffs, planning pressure, recovery, and human judgment.

Evidence discipline

Each serious card states what it proves, what it does not prove, and what should not drift in the next patch.

Human-led AI production

AI accelerates drafts, code, critique, and continuity; the human owner sets direction, judges quality, and approves baselines.

Builder & Method

Built by Kenneth “Ken” Kleveta

This page is also a public proof of process: practical ideas turned into small, inspectable working artifacts.

Back to top

Operator judgment

Marine Corps logistics, business development, audit/readiness, and systems-thinking experience shape what gets built and what gets rejected.

Build small, test fast

The working rhythm is concept → playable loop → smoke test → evidence note → guarded baseline → next proof question.

Public-safe process

The page shows the workflow and proof logic without exposing private prompts, internal scratchpads, or sensitive implementation details.

Collaboration stack: Ken leads product direction, quality judgment, testing, and final calls. ChatGPT, Claude/Fable, and Gemini/SkabGPT support ideation, critique, coding, continuity, and alternate-angle review. GitHub Pages provides a free public deployment layer. The evidence loop keeps the work honest.
