FPS-002: Digital Reality Run

Governed raycaster prototype used to test continuity, drift control, and single-patch discipline.

Review posture: Draft evidence page. Not final. Not approved for publication as a formal claim until Ken reviews the evidence and promotes it into the official record.

1. Exhibit

FPS-002: Digital Reality Run is presented as an evidence exhibit inside the Skab Digital Reality Arcade proof trail. The game is the visible artifact; the review record documents what the build tested, what broke, what changed, and what the work proves or does not yet prove.

2. Initial Question

Can a governed AI-assisted workflow maintain continuity across repeated development cycles without losing the original concept?

3. Root Cause

Repeated AI-assisted iterations were creating drift: vocabulary shifted, mechanics changed, and progress could be confused with rebuild activity. The project needed a controlled way to preserve intent across many development passes.

4. Ken’s Logic

Ken treated the game as a continuity stress test: lock vocabulary, isolate one patch at a time, run a stability check, and stop before uncontrolled expansion. The purpose was not just to make a shooter; it was to prove that AI-assisted execution needs operating doctrine.

5. Observed Failure Modes

  • Intent drift across iterations
  • Over-expansion of mechanics
  • Vocabulary instability
  • Regression risk after improvements
  • Tool/output confusion during long build cycles

6. Corrections Applied

  • Single-patch workflow
  • Locked vocabulary and identity terms
  • Acceptance checks after each patch
  • Explicit stop conditions
  • Quality checkpoint language instead of heavy gate-review framing

7. The Proof Is

  • Playable FPS-style browser artifact
  • Defined controls and run loop
  • Governance language captured in operating skill
  • Milestone-based quality checks
  • Evidence of iteration across sectors, enemies, gates, and recovery rules

8. Finding

Prompt quality mattered, but governance mattered more. Stability improved when the workflow controlled scope, vocabulary, testing, and stop conditions.

9. Relevance Beyond the Game

The same pattern applies to enterprise AI adoption: without governance, outputs can look productive while drifting away from mission intent.
