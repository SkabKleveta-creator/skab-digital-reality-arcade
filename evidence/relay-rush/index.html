Relay Rush

Timing and routing prototype used to test difficulty progression and obstacle growth without breaking theme.

Review posture: Draft evidence page. Not final. Not approved for publication as a formal claim until Ken reviews the evidence and promotes it into the official record.

1. Exhibit

Relay Rush is presented as an evidence exhibit inside the Skab Digital Reality Arcade proof trail. The game is the visible artifact; the review record documents what the build tested, what broke, what changed, and what the work proves or does not yet prove.

2. Initial Question

Can route timing and obstacle pressure create increasing difficulty while staying inside the signal-relay theme?

3. Root Cause

The prototype needed to move beyond a working baseline into paced difficulty without turning into unrelated obstacle spam.

4. Ken’s Logic

Ken used Relay Rush to test theme-consistent difficulty: new obstacles should increase pressure while still reinforcing the idea of relays, timing, signal transfer, and route control.

5. Observed Failure Modes

  • Static difficulty
  • Visual quality limitations
  • Risk of generic obstacles
  • Potential mismatch between theme and challenge

6. Corrections Applied

  • Difficulty progression request
  • Theme-consistent obstacle design
  • Visual readability improvements
  • Handoff prompt discipline

7. The Proof Is

  • Playable HTML prototype
  • Visual enhancement trail
  • Difficulty design pass
  • Evidence page and review seed

8. Finding

Difficulty is not just more hazards. Good progression teaches the player the system under pressure.

9. Relevance Beyond the Game

This is relevant to training and change adoption: escalation should reinforce the operating model, not distract from it.
