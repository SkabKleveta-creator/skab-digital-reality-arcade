AROUND THE FIRE

FIRST KIN

One community. Two scales.

Settlement: place a shelter or service on open ground. Connect it to the hearth with paths. Assign Build to finish it.

People: choose morning and afternoon work. Watching a skilled person teaches understanding. Practice turns that understanding into ability.

Field: fish by pulling when the rod bends. On solo hunts, tap ground to move and use Strike within range. For group hunts, position people and choose roles before committing.

Region: assign Scout to discover nearby places. Study discoveries to unlock crops, bows, trade and regional outposts.

Growth: make room and keep food in reserve to welcome families. Council shows the conditions for a tribe and village.

Controls: tap or click. Drag to pan the map; use + and − to zoom. Solo hunt also supports arrow keys/WASD and Space to strike. Space pulls the fishing line. Esc cancels building placement.
