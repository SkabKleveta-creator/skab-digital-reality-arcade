Operation GATOR RAID

Mission brief before planning begins

MISSION FIRSTPlanning Gate
Mission

Conduct an expeditionary movement from ship to shore, establish a functioning beachhead, preserve command continuity through disruption, recover degraded capability, and enable the force to continue to the final objective.

Commander's Intent

Maintain the force's ability to continue the operation. Organization, communication, sustainment, recovery, and adaptation matter more than avoiding every setback.

Operational Picture

The force begins afloat, crosses water and surf, organizes at the beachhead, establishes support and recovery functions, then advances inland under abstract pressure and friction.

Planning Guidance

Build a plan that preserves command continuity, supports recovery, maintains cohesion, and creates enough operational flexibility to continue after the plan is disrupted.

Planning Assumption — Disruption Is Expected

You do not know exactly when, where, or how disruption will occur. The goal is not to create a perfect plan. The goal is to create a plan that can absorb disruption, recover, and continue.

Success Criteria
Reach the final objective
Maintain meaningful C2 continuity
Preserve cohesion and sustainment
Execute recovery when degraded
Adapt without losing mission purpose
What This Exercise Evaluates
Planning and sequencing
Posture and tempo choices
Support and recovery allocation
Adaptation under disruption
Learning through AAR
Core Rule

Success is not avoiding disruption. Success is adapting to disruption while still accomplishing the mission.
