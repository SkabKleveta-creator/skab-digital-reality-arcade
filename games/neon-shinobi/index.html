NEON SHINOBI // CONTROLS

D-Pad: left/right move. ▲ jumps, ▼ dashes on the ground or slams while airborne. One continuous ground — no lane layers. Combat never locks the route.

A: jump and double-jump onto optional platforms; the ground remains continuous. B: slash. Hold A+B for the launcher.

X: dash on the ground or slam while airborne. Y: trigger Rage when charged.

Keyboard remains active. Standard browser gamepads use the same control path.
