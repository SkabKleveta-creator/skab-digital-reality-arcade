Slide 1

What this is

Plain language

TRACE CHAIN is a serious-play board for asking whether a green-looking audit/control status is actually backed by evidence.

Why it exists

It turns control discipline into player decisions: move fast, verify, document, accept risk, or slow down to preserve truth.

Slide 2

Core loop

Receive pressure: A mission/control situation demands movement.
Choose evidence behavior: Verify, document, defer, route, or push forward.
Absorb consequence: Weak evidence becomes validation debt; strong evidence costs time.
Resolve finding/CAP: Bad assumptions become findings; disciplined recovery becomes corrective action.
Run AAR: State what was proven, what was assumed, and what must change.

Slide 4

Decision pressure

Speed

Moving fast can satisfy the visible mission but create hidden validation debt.

Evidence

Evidence costs time, but it protects the decision from becoming theater.

Recovery

Findings are not failure if the player can trace the cause, correct the control, and capture the lesson.

Slide 6

After-action review prompts

Player report

  • What claim did you accept first?
  • What evidence changed your mind?
  • Where did validation debt appear?
  • What would you do differently next run?

Proof question

Can a first-time player understand the decision pressure in one round and explain the difference between green status and validated status?
