    WHAT THE WILD TEACHES

    Relics of the hunt

    Bring discoveries to a campfire to unlock trail skills. Equip one at a time; relics are kept, never spent.

    Trail skills

    Your discoveries

    One relic in each land
      01 / Find your footing

      Move with A/D or the arrows. Hold Space to jump higher; release for a short hop. On touch, use the arrows and Jump.

      02 / Read the animal

      Hold J or Strike to swing. Watch a predator's warning before it lunges. Press K, Shift, or Evade to roll through danger; evasion needs stamina.

      03 / Carry what matters

      Food restores health. A new club restores durability. If the club breaks, your fists still work. Jump onto small creatures; tigers and the Great Beast cannot be stomped.

      04 / Remember the fire

      Passing a campfire records your resources and the world. Retry and Continue return to the last camp, including the supplies you had then. Returning gives you a brief moment of protection. Reaching a new land also saves.

      05 / Follow your curiosity

      Each land holds a named relic. Secure finds at the next camp to unlock trail skills in Relics & skills. Equip one skill at a time. Raised stone shelves offer optional paths. Ordinary animals may be avoided; the Great Beast must be defeated to finish the hunt.

      06 / Make it home

      Reach the trail marker at the end of each land. Ten lands make one journey. The Great Beast alternates charges and ground slams. Evade a charge. When it raises its forelegs, get ready; hold Jump when the warning says HOLD JUMP NOW. Strike during recovery.

      07 / Watch the ground

      Falling stone and steam vents warn before they strike. Leave a rockfall marker; jump over a steam burst or wait for it to settle. The ground markings show the danger zone.
