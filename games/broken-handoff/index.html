What it is

Broken Handoff is a tabletop/mobile-lite procedure for testing decision quality when a system looks green but the underlying handoff may still be broken.

Current status

Display-worthy validation lane. This index page gives the Arcade a working destination while the fuller mobile validation shell or trial export is staged.

What it proves

Meaning loss, handoff breakdown, and false-green status can be turned into playable decision validation instead of just documentation language.

Still unproven

Whether one player can complete the validation shell and produce a useful after-action review without facilitator friction.

Quick Run Structure

1. Observe: Read the reported green status and compare it to what the system evidence actually supports.
2. Diagnose: Identify where meaning was lost: owner, evidence, timing, validation, or assumption.
3. Stabilize: Choose the least-damaging action that preserves human judgment and prevents blind escalation.
4. Validate: The handoff is not fixed until evidence confirms the receiving side can act correctly.
5. AAR: Capture what was misunderstood, what was proven, and what should change next time.
Emergency reopen rule: One reopened decision may be allowed per run, but it costs Strain +1 and must be recorded in the AAR.

Guardrail

Do not overbuild before the first real AAR. Preserve simple one-thumb/mobile-lite procedure and the controlled undo model.
