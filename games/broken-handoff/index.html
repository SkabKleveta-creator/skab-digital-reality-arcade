Slide 1

What this is

Plain language

Broken Handoff is a tabletop/mobile-lite validation trial about a system that looks repaired but may still be broken underneath.

Why it exists

It tests whether players preserve judgment when the visible status is green but the evidence, owner, or receiving side is not aligned.

Slide 2

Core loop

Observe: Read the reported status and compare it to actual evidence.
Diagnose: Identify where meaning was lost: owner, evidence, timing, validation, or assumption.
Stabilize: Choose the least-damaging action that preserves human judgment.
Route / repair: Move the handoff to the correct owner or rebuild the missing evidence path.
Validate: The handoff is not fixed until the receiving side can act correctly.

Slide 3

Slide 4

Controlled undo model

Before lock

The player may review and reconsider before locking a decision. This supports one-thumb/mobile-lite play without punishing careful reading.

After lock

Allow one emergency Reopen Last Decision per run. It costs Strain +1 and must be recorded in the AAR as “Decision reopened.”

Slide 5

Guardrails

Do not overbuild before first real AAR. The first proof is whether the procedure produces useful learning.
Do not turn it into a full platform yet. No scenario builder, campaign layer, or bloated system before validation.
Do not make the right answer obvious. Options must be similar in length and plausibility to avoid answer-key leakage.
Preserve the core lesson. Repair is not real until validated.

Slide 6

After-action review prompts

Player report

  • What did the green status hide?
  • Where was the handoff actually broken?
  • What evidence changed the decision?
  • Did the receiving side validate the repair?
  • Was the emergency reopen used?

Proof question

Can one player complete the validation shell and produce a useful AAR in one clean run?
